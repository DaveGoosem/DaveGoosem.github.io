## Found a mistake in a post?

This site is open source. If you spot an error, a broken link or something that's out of date, you can [open an issue on the site's GitHub repository](https://github.com/DaveGoosem/DaveGoosem.github.io/issues). Corrections are always welcome, and they help everyone who finds the post later.

I read every message, but this is a personal blog written alongside a full-time role, so replies can take a few days.
